## Module 1 — Who we are, who you talk to
**Who we drive.** Maz Services drives kids who need extra help to and from school.
**Our partners.** We work with two partner companies: FirstAlt and EverDriven. They send us the rides.
**Two people you talk to.** You only talk to two people about a ride: Maz dispatch, and the partner's dispatch. No one else.
You never call a parent. You never call the school. That is not your job.
**Partner dispatch may call you.** FirstAlt or EverDriven dispatch might call you directly about a ride. Answer the call and do what they say.
**Then tell Maz.** After you talk to the partner's dispatch, call or message Maz dispatch too. Keep us in the loop.
**Need dispatch?** Call the dispatch number (placeholder until the dedicated line is built).
**Pay questions.** Questions about your pay or your rate go to Z. Not to dispatch.

## Module 2 — Getting onboarded
You already did most of this to get here. This is a quick map of the whole path, so you understand each piece.
**FirstAlt: step 1.** You get an invite link from FirstAlt to start your file.
**FirstAlt: step 2.** FirstAlt runs your background check. Have your last 7 years of addresses and your last 3 years of work ready — they ask for both.
**FirstAlt: step 3.** You sign a drug test consent form. Then Priority Solutions calls to book your drug test at Concentra, before your first ride.
**FirstAlt: step 4.** You take the FirstAlt online class.
**FirstAlt: step 5.** You upload your license, registration, insurance, and photos of your vehicle. Every document needs its expiry date on it.
**FirstAlt: step 6.** You sign the Acumen contract, take this course, then sign the Maz contract.
**EverDriven track.** EverDriven runs a different path. Here it is, step by step.
**EverDriven: step 1.** You register in the Contractor Compliance app and upload your license, registration, and insurance.
**EverDriven: step 2.** You get fingerprinted for a background check and take an in-person drug and alcohol test.
**EverDriven: step 3.** Your vehicle passes a 50-point inspection, and a mechanic signs off on it.
**EverDriven: step 4.** You take the Hallo English test — about 10 minutes, 5 speaking questions.
**EverDriven: step 5.** You take the SafeRide online safety course — about 4.5 hours.
**EverDriven: step 6.** You set up your banking, install the EverDriven driver app, then pick up your vest and window sticker.
**You are your own business.** You are paid as a 1099 contractor, not an employee. No taxes are taken out of your pay.
**The W-9 form.** You fill out a W-9 form. Pick "Individual/sole proprietor" unless you have an LLC.

## Module 3 — Your vehicle and gear
**Registration, insurance, inspection.** Keep all three current. If one expires, you cannot drive until it's fixed. No exceptions.
**Vest and placard.** Wear your vest. Put your placard in the window. Every ride, every day.
**Camera, if you have one.** Some drivers have a partner-issued camera in their van. If you were given one, keep it on for every ride.

## Module 4 — Accepting a ride
**Read the notes.** Before you accept, read the ride notes. They tell you about a booster seat, an allergy, or how the child likes things done.
**The window.** The app lets you accept a ride about 75 minutes before pickup.
**Accept fast.** Accept the moment the ride opens. Don't wait.
**If you don't accept.** Dispatch will text you, then call you. Too many calls and you can lose routes.

## Module 5 — Running the ride, step by step
**Step 1: Start.** Tap Start in the app the moment you leave for pickup.
**Step 2: drive the route.** Follow the route the app gives you.
**Step 3: arrive.** Get to the pickup spot on time — early is on time.
**EverDriven: tap At Pickup.** When you arrive, tap At Pickup. This tells dispatch you made it. A lot of drivers skip this — don't be one of them.
**Student doesn't come out.** Call dispatch. Never call the school.
**No-load steps.** Wait 5 to 10 minutes — the school district sets the exact time — then call dispatch.
**No-load: what dispatch tells you.** Dispatch tells you to mark it a no-load in the app. You still get full pay.
**Step 4: drop-off.** Only drop the student at the exact stop you were given. Never a different spot, even if asked.
**Step 5: End.** Tap End in the app while you are still at the drop-off spot. Then you can close the app.
**Why the exact spot matters.** Tapping Start or End from the wrong place can look like a fake ride, and dispatch may have to take that pay back.

## Module 6 — Using the app
**The tap most drivers miss.** The At Pickup tap is easy to miss. Tap it every time you arrive — it's your proof that you showed up.
**If the app won't start a ride.** Take a screenshot of the screen.
**Then.** Call dispatch before you drive. Never drive a ride the app can't track.
**Start and end in the zone.** Always tap Start and End while you're inside the pickup or drop-off zone, not from your driveway or down the street.

## Module 7 — The six driving rules
1. **Accept on time.** When the app offers your ride, accept it as soon as you can. See the Accepting a ride module for the exact window.
2. **Arrive on time.** Being early is on time. Being right on time is cutting it close.
3. **Straight there, straight home.** No stops — not for gas, not for coffee, not for errands. Fill your tank before your route starts.
4. **No eating while you drive.** Water and coffee are fine. No eating. The real rule: nothing that takes your eyes or your hands off driving.
5. **No detours.** Follow the route the app gives you. If the road is blocked, call dispatch — don't decide on your own.
6. **Never speed.** Every ride is tracked. Speeding shows up.

## Module 8 — The children
**Greet and settle.** Greet the child by name. Use the same seat every day if the child prefers it — routine is comfort.
**Car seats and boosters.** If the notes say a child needs a car seat or booster, use it every time. Never skip it.
**If a child has a hard moment** (crying, shouting, won't stay seated): pull over somewhere safe and call dispatch.
**What you never do.** Never discipline. Never grab. Never argue. Dispatch handles it from there.
**Never leave a child alone.** Never leave a child alone in the vehicle, for any reason, for any amount of time.
**Exact stop only.** Never drop a child anywhere but the exact stop you were given, even if someone asks you to.
**Privacy.** What happens in the car stays private. No photos of children. No posts about your riders, ever.

## Module 9 — When something goes wrong
- **Someone is hurt or in danger:** call 911 first. Then call dispatch.
- **Anything else:** call dispatch first. Not 911.
- **Accident: write it down** within 24 hours. Just the facts.
- **Sick or can't drive:** call dispatch the second you know — even at 5am.
- **Late in general:** call dispatch before pickup time is due, not after.

## Module 10 — How you get paid
**Pay is per ride.** Pay is set per ride and per route by Z. It's not per hour, and it's not per mile.
**Weekly pay.** You get paid weekly.
**There is a delay.** You do not get paid the same week you drive. Every ride is checked and counted first. That takes time.
**The rule.** Rides you drive Monday to Friday are paid by direct deposit on the Friday two weeks later.
**Example.** You drive Monday, September 14 to Friday, September 18. That money lands on Friday, October 2.
**Your first three Fridays.** New driver? Your first Friday: nothing yet. Second Friday: nothing yet. Third Friday: your first pay. After that, every Friday.
**Your stub comes first.** A few days before the money lands, you get a pay stub by email. It shows the week, your rides, and your total.
**Under $100.** If you earn under $100 in a week, it is not lost. It is added to the next week.
**Held back.** If your stub shows money held back, Z will tell you why.
**If you spot a mistake.** Reply to the stub email or message Z. Give her the ride date and the route name.
**Mistakes get fixed** the next pay cycle.
**Who to ask.** Pay and rate questions go to Z. Not to dispatch.

## Module 11 — Cancellations, no-shows, no-loads
**Late cancel.** Ride cancelled 1 to 2 hours before it starts: you still get full pay.
**No-load wait.** Student not there? Wait 5 to 10 minutes — the school district sets the exact time.
**No-load steps.** Call dispatch. Dispatch tells you to mark it a no-load in the app.
**No-load pay.** You still get full pay for a no-load.
**Driver no-show.** You don't show up for a ride: no pay, and a talk about your contract.

## Module 12 — Your scorecard
**Six numbers.** Every week you get six numbers: acceptance, on-time start, on-time arrival, on-time completion, responsiveness, reliability.
**What they mean.** How fast you accept, whether you start, arrive, and finish on time, how fast you answer dispatch, and whether dispatch can count on you.
**Why it matters.** Drivers dispatch never has to chase get the most routes.
**Low numbers** mean fewer routes, then a conversation.
**No ranks.** There are no ranks or labels. Just your six numbers.

## Module 13 — Staying current
**What expires.** Your registration, insurance, inspection, drug test, and background check all expire.
**Reminders** come from dispatch, or from Z.
**Renew on time.** Renew the week you're told. Not the last day.
**If something expires:** no driving until it's fixed. No exceptions.
